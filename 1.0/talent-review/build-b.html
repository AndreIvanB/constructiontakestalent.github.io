How we work

What you get that a job board can’t give you

01

We read the scope, not the title.

“Superintendent” means something different on a tilt-up than it does on a hospital. We ask what you actually built, who reported to you, and how the job was delivered — then we match on that.

02

Your name doesn’t move without your say-so.

Nothing goes to a client until you’ve seen the company, the project, and the number and told us yes. No blind submittals, no surprise calls to your current employer.

03

Straight answers on comp and travel.

You’ll know the range, the per diem, the vehicle allowance, and how many nights away before you take an interview — not after you’ve burned a PTO day on one.
